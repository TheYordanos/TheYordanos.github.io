Ethio Archive: A Digital Home for Ethiopian Writers

March 23, 2026

Hello :) Here is what I've been working on for the past couple of days. Ethiopian Literary Archive, a web platform dedicated to celebrating the rich literary heritage of Ethiopia. I made this project because I wanted a place that has a full collection of Ethiopian writers and their books neatly put together. Its simply an accessible space where readers can discover Ethiopian authors and their works.

Why an Ethiopian Literary Archive?

Ethiopia has a wonderful literary tradition spanning centuries, from ancient Ge'ez manuscripts to contemporary novels that speak to global audiences. Writers like Haddis Alemayehu, whose masterpiece Fikir Eske Mekabir (Love to the Grave) remains one of the most beloved novels in Amharic literature, alongside voices like Maaza Mengiste, shortlisted for the Booker Prize, and Dinaw Mengestu, a MacArthur Fellow.

Yet, there hasn't been a dedicated digital space that brings these voices together in an elegant, accessible way. The Ethiopian Literary Archive aims to fill that gap.

What You'll Find

A Curated Collection of Ethiopian Authors

I've started with a carefully selected group of Ethiopian writers, spanning generations and writing in both Amharic and English. Each author has their own dedicated page featuring:

  • A biographical sketch
  • Complete bibliography
  • External links for further reading
  • A featured work to get you started

Detailed Book Information

Every book in our archive includes:

  • Full descriptions and synopses
  • Publication details (year, publisher, page count)
  • Genre classification
  • Series information (when applicable)

A Design Inspired by Vintage Libraries

The site's aesthetic draws from old paper and map textures. Warm, neutral tones with serif typography that feels both timeless and inviting. I wanted the experience of browsing the archive to feel like exploring a physical library, with the convenience of a modern digital experience.

Thoughtful Features for Discovery

  • Search: Quickly find authors by name
  • Responsive design: Beautiful on desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Related authors: Discover new writers connected to your favorites
  • Series grouping: See how books connect within a series
  • External links: Connect to Wikipedia, Goodreads, and author websites

Explore the Archive

I invite you to explore the Ethiopian Literary Archive and discover (or rediscover) the voices that have shaped Ethiopian literature. Whether you're a longtime reader or new to Ethiopian authors, there's something here for everyone.

Visit the archive: etharchive.github.io

For Writers, Publishers, and Scholars

If you're an Ethiopian author (or know one) who would like to be included in the archive, or if you're a publisher with suggestions for our collection, we'd love to hear from you. The Ethiopian Literary Archive is a community resource, and we're committed to representing the full diversity of Ethiopian literary voices.

Contact: ethioarchive@gmail.com

The Ethiopian Literary Archive is an ongoing project, built with love and respect for Ethiopia's literary heritage. We hope it becomes a valuable resource for readers, students, and anyone interested in the power of storytelling from the Horn of Africa.
